KaamFlow Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026 · App: KaamFlow · Publisher: TinkerDock Studio

This Privacy Policy explains how KaamFlow, published by TinkerDock Studio, handles information when you use the Android application. Questions: tinkerdock.support@gmail.com.

1. Information you enter into KaamFlow

You may enter business and customer names, telephone numbers, addresses, GST identification numbers, quotes, invoices, jobs, prices, amounts due, payment records, photographs, business branding, and related documents. The app stores these business records primarily on your device, using local application storage and a SQLite database. TinkerDock Studio does not operate a cloud database for these business records in this release and does not provide automatic KaamFlow multi-device synchronization.

2. Optional device access

  • Contacts: With your permission and an action you initiate, KaamFlow may read selected contacts to help populate customer records.
  • Photos and files: You may choose job photographs, a logo, documents, or backups for local app functionality.
  • Notifications: With permission, the app may show local reminders and reschedule reminders after your device restarts.

These local actions do not themselves mean that TinkerDock Studio receives a copy of your contact book or photos.

3. Google services and information transferred off-device

  • Advertising: In the Free tier, optional rewarded advertising may be delivered through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). This SDK can process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, interactions with ads, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Google and its advertising partners may process data in accordance with their policies. Ads do not appear in the Pro tier according to the app's current design.
  • Google Play Billing: Google Play processes optional Pro purchases and subscriptions. KaamFlow uses purchase status and related identifiers to enable or restore paid functionality. Payment-card details are processed by Google Play, not entered into KaamFlow's own payment form.
  • Optional Google Sign-In: If the sign-in feature is available and you choose to use it, Google may authenticate you and provide your profile name, email address, account identifier, and profile image. The current app stores related profile information locally. Signing in is not needed to use the main offline business features, and it does not enable cloud synchronization of your business records.

For more about Google's data practices, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Exports, sharing and device backups

When you export an app backup, share a PDF invoice or quotation, share a reminder, or send files to another app, the destination you choose may receive the contents. You are responsible for selecting trusted recipients and securely storing exported backups.

Android or device-manufacturer backup and transfer services may also back up application data, depending on your device, Google account and system settings. This can include locally stored records. These system backups are separate from a KaamFlow-operated cloud service.

5. How information is used

Information is used to provide customer and job management, document creation, offline records, reminders, local reports, optional sign-in, ads, subscription entitlements, and troubleshooting of services involved. We do not sell a KaamFlow customer-record database because we do not operate one in this release.

6. Storage, security and retention

Business records are retained on your device until you edit or delete them, clear app data, or uninstall the application. Local device protections and your choice of backup recipients help protect these records. Online communications with Google's services use the security provided by those services. We cannot guarantee that every device, exported file, or third-party destination is secure.

Google may retain data related to advertising, authentication, payments or account activity according to its own policies and legal requirements. TinkerDock Studio cannot remotely view or erase business records stored only on your device.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • You can edit and remove individual local records within the app where those controls are provided.
  • You can remove locally stored KaamFlow app data via Android Settings → Apps → KaamFlow → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling KaamFlow. These actions can permanently erase business records if you have no backup.
  • Delete exported backup files or PDFs from the locations and services where you saved or shared them.
  • You can stop optional Google Sign-In and manage your connected Google Account permissions through Google.
  • Manage or cancel Pro subscriptions through Google Play. Cancelling a subscription is separate from clearing local data.
  • Contact tinkerdock.support@gmail.com for privacy questions. We cannot directly delete device-local records that we cannot access.

8. Children

KaamFlow is intended for adult tradespeople and business users, not children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when KaamFlow's features, software development kits, or data practices change. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
