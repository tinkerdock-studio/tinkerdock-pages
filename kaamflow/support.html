KaamFlow Support

Need help with quotes, invoices, backup, Pro access or a technical problem? Email tinkerdock.support@gmail.com. Include your Android version, app version, what you expected, and what happened. Please do not email real customer records, payment-card information, identity documents or your passwords unless specifically required through an appropriate secure channel.

Back up before changing phones

KaamFlow does not offer automatic app-operated cloud sync. Use the in-app Backup & Restore feature to export a backup to a safe location before reinstalling or changing devices, and verify you can restore it. Android or device-vendor backup services may act separately.

Clear locally stored data

  1. Back up anything you want to keep first.
  2. On Android open Settings → Apps → KaamFlow → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall KaamFlow.
  3. Delete any backup files, PDFs or other copies you previously saved or shared separately.

Warning: Clearing storage or uninstalling without a usable backup can permanently remove your customer, job, invoice, and payment records. TinkerDock Studio cannot remotely restore records it never received.

Pro subscriptions

Open the Google Play Store app → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions to review, manage or cancel KaamFlow Pro when available. Subscription cancellation is separate from removal of local data.
